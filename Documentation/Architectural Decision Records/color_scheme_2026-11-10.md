# Color Scheme
## Status: Pending
## Context:
It is important to have a color scheme to ensure consistent themes and presentation throughout the website. This lets users have predictable visual cues and minimize mental load from processing different color schemes and figuring out what they are looking at.

## Decision:
As of November 10th, 2026, the color scheme is as
follows:

- The header bar will be white
- The background of the pages will be #423e37 (Charcoal Brown)
- Text will be white
- Chart border will be #e3b23c (Sunflower Gold)
- Chart background will be #6e675f (Dim Grey)
- Chart lines will be either #edebd7 (Eggshell) or #a39594 (Rosy Granite)

This decision is pending visual tests and subject to modifications
